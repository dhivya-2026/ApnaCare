Attendant or nurse? How to choose

Hi {{first_name}}, one of the most common questions we hear is which kind of caregiver a family needs. Here is the difference.

Care attendant

✓Bathing and personal hygiene
✓Mobility and walking support
✓Meals and medicine reminders
✓Daily WhatsApp updates to family

Registered nurse

✓Sterile wound dressings
✓Injections and IV infusions
✓Catheter and tube feeding care
✓Vital sign charting

Clinical procedures are performed only by registered GNM or B.Sc nurses, on your treating doctor’s prescription. Not sure which fits? Describe the situation and your coordinator will tell you.
